A small-party printable

Potluck Sign-Up Card

A potluck turns the heaviest planning job — all that food — into something the whole room carries together. Print one card, leave it where people will walk by (or drop it in the group chat as a photo), and let good cooks call dibs on good things.

How to use it

  1. Fill the top strip first — what, when, where — then the “Still needed most” line, with the categories you're short on.
  2. Guests pick by category, not by recipe. “A side” is a real answer; family-recipe dishes are a bonus, not a requirement.
  3. Ask people to note anything dietary (nut-free, veg, gluten-free) in the notes column — it keeps the table kind to everyone eating.
  4. As cards fill up, copy each line onto your tally page (page 3). The gaps tell you exactly what host-you covers.
  5. Update the still-needed line as gaps close, re-copy, keep circulating. Crowds don't read; they glance — keep the top line sharp.

What's inside

  • Page 2 — the pass-around card, with a filled-in-helpfully top strip.
  • Page 3 — the host's tally card by category, so nothing arrives uncounted.

A kind note: thank you for carrying a piece of this table. The dish that “always vanishes” at your house counts exactly as much as anything fancy.

Print at 100% (“Actual size”), US Letter, portrait. Prints nicely in black & white. Personal use: print as many copies as you like.
