Six-Week Countdown

A small gathering doesn't need a big machine to organize it — just a gentle pace. This sheet walks you from six weeks out to the day of, one easy focus per week, so the work happens in small forgettable pieces instead of one frantic afternoon.

How to use it

  1. Write your party date at the top of page 2, then mark which week you're already in. Mid-countdown is a perfectly good place to start.
  2. Each week, read its one big job and do that job first. The smaller chores can scatter through the days where they fit.
  3. Tick the box only when the big job is handled. Half-done counts as “in motion” — momentum is allowed on this sheet.
  4. Printed this late in the game? Skip to the week you're actually in. A shorter countdown still counts; it just moves faster.
  5. Keep page 2 on the fridge and fill page 3 as you go — write freely, scratch through anything that stops mattering.

What's inside

  • Page 2 — the countdown at a glance: one focus and one big job per week.
  • Page 3 — week-by-week details with room to write each week's real to-dos.

A kind note: the parties people remember are mostly made of one thing — that you had them at all. The centerpieces will not be remembered. You will be.

Print at 100% (“Actual size”), US Letter, portrait. Prints nicely in black & white. Personal use: print as many copies as you like.
