Guest & Contact Tracker

The guest list is the only part of a party that can't be bought or improvised. One page for every name, the best way to reach them, and what they answered — so the food, the chairs and the parking math later is arithmetic instead of detective work.

How to use it

  1. Start with every name you'd even consider inviting. Lists shrink kindly before invites go out; they can't grow after.
  2. Fill in a phone or an email where you know it. The blanks reveal themselves gently now, and un-gently the day before.
  3. The RSVP column is yours to write in: y (yes), m (maybe), n (no). A maybe kept warm often warms up to yes.
  4. Use the People column for heads, not names — it's what food math runs on when someone brings a plus-one.
  5. When an RSVP arrives, write it that same day. One tidy page beats a tidy memory, every time.

What's inside

  • Page 2 — the tracker grid: names, contact, RSVPs, headcounts, notes.
  • Page 3 — getting everyone there: gentle nudges, dietary summary, rides and parking.

A kind note: an unanswered invite isn't a rejection — it's a busy human. This page keeps track of facts so your feelings can stay out of the arithmetic.
